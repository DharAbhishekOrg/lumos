## 1. Headline

`implemented`, and 13 are `not_started`.** Every one of the 13 has a named

HELLO/HELLO_ACK authenticated capability exchange with zero-false-positive

durations and v2 payload; the feature-permission table with honest denial
copy; the encrypted media object store; proximity in words; App Lock over
schema v3 with the ADR-010 device-auth seam and the ADR-011 screen policy;
and the S6 closing proofs — 110 synthetic peers through the composed
sighting→triage→HELLO→presence path under the connect budget, the TRS-ENV-003
rescan ladder, and the privacy-safe discovery-success metric.

**Three things it did not deliver, stated here rather than in a footnote:**

1. **The Nearby feed UI is not mounted.** SRS-NBY-008..015 and 017 — the feed
   surface, preview retrieval flow, adapter-off states, hide controls,
   skeletons, the one-action stop — are `not_started`. The plumbing they
   mount on is proven; the surfaces are not built. §6.2 names destinations.
2. **Every physical-radio verification is owner-held.** AT-003/004, the
   15-minute rotation capture, the 3-second preview P75, battery and radio
   traces: G-01, §6.4. CI proved the synthetic halves only.
3. **iOS background restoration (TRS-IOS-009) exists only as the scaffold**

   nothing exercises an OS-initiated relaunch, and nothing can from CI.

## 5. Findings for the G-07 cryptographic-review dossier

### 5.1 Ed25519 signing splits three ways by platform, so the seam is the API

WP-02 found Android cannot *generate* Ed25519; WP-03 adds the signing half:
JCA on Android cannot sign with an imported raw key either, and CryptoKit
*randomizes* Ed25519 signatures, so committed-byte vectors cannot pin Apple
output. The resolution is uniform: a one-method `Signer` seam
(`Hello.Signer`, and its Swift closure twin); libsodium signs on Android
devices, JCA signs in JVM tests, and Swift tests verify the committed
signature and round-trip their own. The dossier should confirm the seam
cannot be satisfied by anything but the identity private key.

### 5.2 The 18-byte hint cannot ride a legacy advertisement

ADR-002's Android Service-Data hint (18 bytes under a 128-bit service UUID)
exceeds the legacy 31-byte PDU budget once the UUID is paid for — the
arithmetic has no remainder to argue about. The executed ruling: hint mode
uses LE Extended Advertising; on `FEATURE_UNSUPPORTED`/`DATA_TOO_LARGE` the
controller downgrades honestly to UUID-only mode (the iOS posture) rather
than truncating. Field consequence, G-01-flagged: hinted discovery requires
extended-advertising-capable hardware on the advertising side.

### 5.3 HELLO freshness is per-direction, and replay fails before crypto

Presence is granted only on an ACK answering one's *own* outstanding nonce;
each direction of a link runs its own exchange. A replayed ACK with nothing
outstanding fails with `ERR_HELLO_NONCE_MISMATCH` before any signature
verification runs. The dossier should check the committed vectors' negative
set covers the reviewer's preferred replay orderings.
