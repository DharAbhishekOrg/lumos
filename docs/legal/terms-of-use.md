# Terms of Use

**Document version: 2026-08-19.1**

## The short version

Lumos helps you meet people who are physically near you. It runs on your phone.
Your profile, your messages and your photos stay on your device and travel
directly to the people you have matched with. We cannot read them.

You must be 18 or older to use Lumos. Be decent to people. If someone is not
decent to you, you can block and report them, and you never have to explain why.

## Who can use Lumos

You need to be 18 or older. There is no way around this and no verification
step that lets you in early.

You need to use Lumos as yourself. Do not pretend to be someone else, and do
not make a profile for a person who is not you.

## What we ask of you

Do not use Lumos to harass, threaten, stalk or intimidate anyone. Do not send
sexual content to someone who has not asked for it. Do not post other people's
private information. Do not use Lumos to sell things, run scams, or recruit for
anything.

The [Community Rules](community-rules.md) go into more detail. They are part of
these terms.

## What Lumos does and does not do

Lumos shows you people nearby and lets you talk to them if you both want to. It
does not check who anyone is. A profile is what a person typed about
themselves; nothing more.

Meeting people from the internet carries risk, and meeting people who are
already close to you carries its own kind. Tell someone where you are going.
Meet in public first. Trust your own judgement over anything an app tells you.

## Your account, your device

There is no account. Lumos does not ask for your phone number, your email
address or a login from another service. Your identity is a set of keys created
on your phone, and it exists nowhere else.

That has a consequence worth saying plainly: **if you lose your device or reset
your identity, nobody can restore it for you.** There is no password reset,
because there is no password and no server holding your account. Recovery
setup, if you choose to use it, saves your *content* - not your identity. You
can read exactly what that means in the app before you decide.

## Ending it

You can delete your profile, reset your identity, or wipe everything, at any
time, from inside the app. You do not have to ask us and you do not have to
give a reason.

We can stop someone using Lumos if they break these terms in a way that puts
other people at risk.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, Lumos will show you the new
version and ask you to accept it. The app records which version you accepted,
on your device only.
