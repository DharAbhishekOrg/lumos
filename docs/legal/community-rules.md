# Community Rules

**Document version: 2026-08-19.1**

Lumos puts you in a room with people who are actually near you. That makes it
better than most apps at meeting someone, and it makes the rules matter more.

## Be a person, not a performance

Use your own photos. Say true things about yourself. You do not have to share
anything you do not want to - but what you do share should be real.

## Take no for an answer

The first no is the only one you need. Do not ask again in different words.
Do not send sexual photos or messages to someone who has not said they want
them.

## Do not use nearness as a threat

You know roughly where someone is. That is the point of Lumos, and it is also
the thing that can be turned into a weapon. Do not wait for someone. Do not
follow them. Do not tell them you know where they are.

## Leave people alone when they leave

If someone unmatches or blocks you, that is the end of it. Do not make a new
profile to reach them.

## Do not out anyone

What someone shares with you here about their gender, their orientation or
their life is theirs. Do not screenshot it into somewhere else. Do not tell
people who did not ask.

## Do not sell, scam or recruit

No advertising, no crypto, no "investment opportunity", no asking for money,
no drivers for your cause.

## When something goes wrong

Block first. You do not owe anyone a conversation about it, and blocking is
immediate.

Then report if you want to. Reporting is separate from blocking, it does not
tell the other person anything, and you can do one without the other.

If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services. Lumos is an app;
it cannot come and help you.

## What happens to people who break these rules

Depending on what happened: a warning, losing access to nearby discovery, or
losing access to Lumos. Anything involving a threat to someone's safety goes
straight to the last one.

## Where this document stands

These rules are the product team's position and they are enforceable through
the app's safety tools. The moderation and appeals process that sits behind
them is built in a later release, and the legal review of this text is
owner-executed under SRS-NFR-COM-009 (gate G-09).
