# CR-010: The GATT link's two characteristics and its handshake timeout

- **Status:** **ACCEPTED** by the owner on 2026-11-28, as proposed. Applied in the NB-1 step 2f slice (register, codegen in Go, Java and Swift, the signed-configuration schema and every golden vector that embeds `constants_hash`, in one commit).
- **Executes:** NB-1 step 2. The register change, the codegen and the regenerated vectors land in one commit, before either app's GATT adapter.
- **Raised:** 2026-11-27 by the agent, when NB-1 step 2 found the gap. ADR-002 fixes the service UUID but no characteristics, so two phones can't exchange HELLO.
- **Design:** [ADR-013](../adrs/ADR-013-gatt-link-layout.md).
- **Affected requirements:**
  - TRS-PROTO-017 (trust only at the authenticated HELLO);
  - TRS-TRN-002/003 (paced, serialized GATT operations);
  - TRS-TST-003 (GATT roles, MTU, pacing, disconnect);
  - SRS-NBY-005..007 (discovery, dedup and aging);
  - TRS-CONST-003 (a register change needs an approved CR).

## The change

Three new register values, in the existing sections:

| Name | Section | Value | Overridable | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GATT_LINK_IN_CHAR_UUID` | identity_discovery | `3F8886C9-0258-4A09-B2C6-75461BE6D3C3` | No | The characteristic the peripheral receives on (Write and Write Without Response) |
| `GATT_LINK_OUT_CHAR_UUID` | identity_discovery | `9076B088-D799-41D4-8786-B403D3B309AD` | No | The characteristic the peripheral sends on (Notify) |
| `HELLO_HANDSHAKE_TIMEOUT_SEC` | identity_discovery | default 10, 5 to 30 | Yes | How long a discovery link may take to finish both HELLOs before it is dropped. It keeps the connect budget from being held by a silent or hostile peer |

The UUIDs were generated once, at random, and are fixed from then on, the same as the service UUID.

## What does not change

- The advertisement, the EID schedule and the HELLO/HELLO_ACK layouts.
- The service UUID and the MTU target.
- `MAX_CONCURRENT_GATT_LINKS` and every safety rule.

## Owner decision

**Accept or reject.** Accepting lets both apps build the connection step, so two phones can find each other. Rejecting means the agent proposes another layout. There is no option of leaving the identifiers out of the register.
