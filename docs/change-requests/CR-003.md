# CR-003: Share a location in a matched chat

- **Status:** **ACCEPTED** by the owner on 2026-11-22 ("CR-003, location
  sharing: accept"), with guardrails 1–3, 5 and 6 as written. **Guardrail 4
  (route) is still open:** the owner has not yet chosen (a) or (b). Nothing
  has been built; it executes in WP-07.
- **Raised:** 2026-11-21, at the WP-05 kickoff, by the owner ("like whatsapp
  you can send media location"). Drafted by the implementing agent.
- **Affected requirements:** none today. This adds a capability the
  baseline does not have. Neighbouring rules it must respect:
  - SRS-PRO-005: location metadata is stripped from shared images.
  - SRS-NBY-016 and SRS-NFR-COM-008: no exact-location claims.
  - SRS-DAT-006, SRS-NFR-PRI-005 and TRS-OBS-002: no exact location in
    telemetry, analytics or logs.
  - SRS-MED-003: nothing reaches a person before mutual acceptance.
- **Proposed home:** WP-07 (nearby media), next to photos and files. It is a
  chat attachment of the same kind.

## What was asked

A WhatsApp-like "send location" attachment in a matched chat. (Photos, files
and voice notes are already baselined in WP-07, Bluetooth/Wi-Fi only; this CR
covers location alone.)

## Why this is a ruling, not a slice

In a dating app, a precise location sent to someone met an hour ago is a
safety decision, and the baseline currently goes the other way. It strips
location from photos and keeps exact location out of every surface. Adding the
feature is reasonable, but the guardrails have to be chosen deliberately.

## Proposed guardrails (for the owner to accept, change or reject)

1. **One-shot only.** A single pin per explicit send. No live location
   tracking in 1.0.
2. **Matched chats only, behind the ConsentGate.** A location becomes a
   conversation class: it never flows before mutual acceptance and is refused
   after an unmatch or block, like text.
3. **Explicit confirmation every time,** with a preview of the pin before it
   is sent, and an option to send an approximate area instead of the exact
   point.
4. **Route.** Either:
   - (a) nearby-only like other media, which is the safest; or
   - (b) also over the relay as end-to-end encrypted text, since it is a few
     bytes and the relay only sees ciphertext.

   **The owner picks one.**
5. **Never stored by the backend, logged or sent to analytics.** This already
   follows from TRS-BCK-002 and TRS-OBS-002.
6. **The recipient can delete it locally,** and the sender can delete it for
   everyone through the AMD-005 event envelope.

## Ruling

- 2026-11-22: accepted, guardrails 1–3, 5 and 6 as written.
- Still needed before WP-07 builds it: guardrail 4, route (a) or (b).

On acceptance, the SRS gains new numbered requirements and the matrix gains
entries, through the same change-request process as CR-001 and CR-002.
