- **Status:** **PROPOSED** 2026-11-23, awaiting the owner's ruling on the four
  points in §5. Nothing is applied in either app yet.
- **Executes:** the chat-surface slice (the next slice after the WP-05
  closeout), Android then iOS. That slice turns the tokens into the
  platform theme files and builds the first screens on them.
- **Raised:** 2026-11-23 by the owner: "take example of award winning UI/UX
  apps and adopt the design theme ... easy to use, fantastic theme ... we
  have to be super careful with UI/UX engineering."
- **Affected requirements:**
  - SRS-UX-001..016, TRS-UX-001..012 and SRS-NFR-A11Y-001..010;
  - SRS-RMT-005 (ticks);
  - SRS-MAT-008 (the match code);
  - SRS-SAF-001 (safety from every context).
- **Why a CR:** SRS-UX-003 and TRS-UX-002 fix the theme by name and palette

## 1. What it is

A design system and seven key screens, both saved as artifacts the owner can
open and comment on:

The tokens are copied to `docs/design/midnight-aurora.tokens.json`, which
becomes the TRS-UX-001 shared token source once this CR is accepted.

## 2. Where it comes from (researched 2026-11-23)

- **Apple's Liquid Glass (iOS 26)** is reserved for the navigation layer
  above content. Apple's own guidance says not to use it in the content
  layer, and it drew public criticism for legibility. Lumos uses glass for
  the tab bar, toolbars and sheets only.
- **Material 3 Expressive (Android 16)** is Google's most researched design
  update. Expressive designs were rated more positive and friendly, and
  preferred by 18–24-year-olds. Lumos uses it on Android.
- **Apple Design Awards 2026:** the Interaction and Inclusivity winners are
  platform-tailored and accessible by construction. That is the bar.
- **Hinge** ("designed to be deleted"): optimistic, conversational,
  prompt-led profiles.
- **Airbnb's 2025 redesign:** warmth and dimensional icons.
- **Google Play's Best App of 2025:** "disconnect to reconnect".

## 3. The decisions

1. **Native-first on each platform** (SRS-UX-004), with one shared token
   source.
2. **Glass only on the navigation layer,** at least 84% opaque in Midnight.
   At 78%, tab labels measured 4.3:1 over a bright photo and failed AA; at
   84% they measure 5.1:1. Reduce Transparency makes it fully opaque.
3. **Every accent means one thing:**
   - violet: brand and primary actions;
   - aqua: nearby and live;
   - coral: the match;
   - amber: venues and caution;
   - danger: destructive actions, always with an icon and a word;
   - blue: read ticks, per the 2026-11-21 ruling.
4. **Safety one tap away,** in the same top-trailing place on every screen
   that shows a person. The sheet states what the other person will and
   won't see.
5. **Consent stated at the point of action:** "Aanya only finds out if you
   are both interested."
6. **No engagement dark patterns:** no streaks, no "who viewed you", no
   fake urgency. These are also a legal risk under the EU DSA and consumer
   law.
7. **Motion and haptics only at three moments:** the match, in-person
   verification and a completed transfer (SRS-UX-009). Reduced motion is
   honoured.

## 4. Verification done

- **Every colour token pair** used for text, icons or control borders was
  computed against WCAG 2.2 in both themes. Four failed in the first draft
  (the dark control border, the light outline on canvas, the light read tick
  on the violet bubble, and dark danger on `surface-high`); all were fixed.
- **Every extra pair the screens use** was computed too (24 pairs). One
  failed, the glass case above, and was fixed.
- **Touch targets** are 44 pt on iPhone and 48 dp on Android throughout.
- **Every state has a word,** not colour alone: ticks, "Here now", "Nearby
  only", "Not sent".
- **Not done:** a usability test with real users, and screen-reader passes
  on devices. Both come with the built screens (WP-10 owns the full audit).

## 5. For the owner's ruling

1. The direction as a whole (§3) and the token values.
2. **Daylight,** a light theme beside Midnight. The baseline names only
   Midnight Aurora.
3. **Sora** for display text: SIL OFL 1.1, which has copyleft-style terms
   for modified font files. It would ship unmodified. AGENTS.md requires your
   explicit approval for any copyleft licence. The alternative is the
   platform display fonts.
4. The interest action as the primary violet button, with coral reserved for
   the match moment.
